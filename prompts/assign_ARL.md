## ARL — Acceptance Readiness Level

Core question: **Will society/the sector accept it?**

| Bin | Levels | Phase | Meaning | Key question |
|-----|--------|-------|---------|--------------|
| Low | 1–3 | Resistance | Seen as illegitimate/controversial/unwanted by large groups of the population | Does society actively reject it? |
| Mid | 4–6 | Hesitation | Questionable/inappropriate to groups of the population or key sector actors | Are stakeholders skeptical? |
| High | 7–9 | Legitimacy | Only marginal groups object, or generally accepted | Is it broadly accepted? |

Strong evidence types: public controversy or endorsement, NGO/advocacy positions, opinion surveys, media sentiment, protests.
Typical sources: news, NGO reports, social commentary, surveys.

Note: ARL evidence is often scarce. "No controversy found" may mean High ARL *or* an obscure technology nobody has opinions on yet. When no direct evidence exists, set evidence_gap to true and explain what was inferred.

Also consider as acceptance evidence: industry association endorsements, extension service recommendations, voluntary adoption at scale, consumer purchasing trends, and government support programs. Formal opinion surveys are valuable but not required — widespread voluntary adoption and institutional endorsement are themselves strong signals of acceptance.
